MEET THE PERSON BEHIND THE CAMERA

About Saksham.

Hi, I'm Saksham — the person behind the camera at Saksham's Studio.

For me, photography is more than just taking pictures. It is a way of connecting with people, understanding their stories and preserving the moments that matter most.

I believe the most beautiful photographs happen naturally. My approach is simple — keep things comfortable, genuine and effortless, so every photograph reflects the real emotions of the moment.

Whether it is a wedding, pre-wedding celebration, event or personal portrait, I focus on capturing genuine emotions, meaningful details and the little moments that might otherwise be forgotten.

At Saksham's Studio, every photograph is created with creativity, patience and attention to detail — because your memories deserve to be preserved beautifully.
